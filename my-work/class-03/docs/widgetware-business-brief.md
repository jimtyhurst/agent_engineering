# WidgetWare Business Brief
WidgetWare is a B2B software company.

# Products
WidgetWare sells a configurable platform that helps manufacturing and industrial organizations connect fragmented plant systems, digitize manual workflows, improve operational visibility, and introduce AI-assisted decisions without replacing every legacy investment at once.

# Target Customers
WidgetWare sells to:

- Companies that are involved in manufacturing, industrial automation, automotive manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, and industrial equipment.
- Companies that are mid-market to large enterprise.
- Companies located in:
  - United States
  - Europe
  - India 

WidgetWare does _not_ sell to:

- Very small firms.
- Purely consumer businesses.
- Unsupported geographic regions.
- Companies without relevant industrial operations.

# Buying Signals
WidgetWare is interested in the following buying signals:

- New AI leadership
- Digital transformation program
- Plant modernization
- Smart factory initiative
- Manufacturing data initiative
- Investment in new automation or control systems

# The SDR Process
The Sales Development Representative (SDR) prepares a high-quality opportunity for the next step in the sales process. The SDR researches potential customers, qualifies them based on WidgetWare's Ideal Customer Profile, and prepares an outreach context.

Typical outputs are:

- Clean account notes.
- A buyer hypothesis.
- An approved outreach draft.
- A qualified meeting. 

# Human Approval

All external actions must be reviewed and approved by a human. This includes:

- Outbound email.
- Updates to the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system data.
